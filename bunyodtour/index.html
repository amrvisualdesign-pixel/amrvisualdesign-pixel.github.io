Questions before you book

When is the best time for the Pamir Highway?

From June to September the high passes are usually open and the weather is stable. The Fann Mountains are good from May to October.

Do I need a special permit for the Pamirs?

Yes. The Pamirs (GBAO) need a separate permit in addition to your visa. We help you arrange it together with the route.

How do I get a visa?

Many travelers apply for Tajikistan's e-Visa online before the trip. The rules depend on your passport, so check them for your country.

Can the route be changed?

Yes. Every tour is private, so the days, the stops and the pace are set around you.
